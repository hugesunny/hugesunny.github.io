I study organic semiconductors and organic field-effect transistors (OFETs) that
retain electronic performance under mechanical deformation.

Research topics include strain-dependent structural evolution, charge transport,
backbone flexibility, crosslinking strategies, and interface engineering for
deformable organic semiconductor devices.
