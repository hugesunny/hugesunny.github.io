I investigate how molecular structure, backbone rigidity, molecular packing, and
thin-film microstructure determine charge transport and mechanical behavior in
conjugated polymer semiconductors.

My work uses multiscale structural and electrical characterization to connect
molecular-level deformation with device-scale electronic performance.
