      text: |-
        My research focuses on understanding and controlling the relationships among
        **molecular structure, thin-film morphology, interfaces, and charge transport**
        in conjugated polymers for emerging organic electronic devices.

        ### Structure-Property Relationships in Conjugated Polymers
        I investigate how backbone structure, molecular packing, thin-film
        microstructure, and mechanical deformation govern charge transport in
        conjugated polymer semiconductors.

        ### Deformable Organic Electronics
        I study mechanically deformable organic semiconductors and organic
        field-effect transistors (OFETs), with emphasis on strain-dependent structure,
        charge transport, and interface engineering.

        ### Organic Photodetectors and Electrochemical Transistors
        My current work includes near-infrared and short-wave infrared organic
        photodetectors (OPDs), as well as organic electrochemical transistors (OECTs),
        using molecular and interface engineering to control electronic and
        mixed ionic-electronic transport.
